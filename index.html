Who We Are

Our Mission

K9 Honor Society is committed to supporting military veterans and law enforcement officers by uniting them with exceptional four-legged companions. We believe the transition from active service—and the weight carried after the badge or uniform comes off—should never be navigated alone. Through careful temperament matching, structured preparation, and dedicated placement, we build lasting partnerships grounded in loyalty, purpose, and mutual trust.

You Are Not Alone

After service, you don't have to walk alone.

K9 Honor Society helps military veterans and law enforcement officers, people who gave years of their lives to serving others, find a dog to share the rest of theirs with. A loyal friend who is happy to see you every single day, through the easy days and the hard ones.

No one who served should ever feel they are facing life alone.

Built onLoyalty★Trust★Purpose★Honor

  • A friend who is always glad to see you

    Coming home from service can feel quiet. A loyal companion brings warmth, a daily rhythm, and a reason to smile back into every day.

  • Chosen together, never just assigned

    You are not simply handed a dog. We get to know you, your home, your pace, and your hopes, then help you find the dog that feels like yours.

  • Ready for everyday life

    Every dog is carefully vetted and trained with purpose, and we help you both settle in together, so life at home feels natural from the very first day.

  • By your side for life

    Placement day is only the beginning. We stay close with check-ins, answers, and encouragement for as long as you and your dog need us.

Our Programs

Who We Serve

Military Veterans

You carried the mission. We help make sure you never carry what comes after alone. We unite military veterans with a carefully vetted, purpose-trained canine companion matched to your temperament, lifestyle, and needs, and we stand behind the partnership for life.

Learn More about our program for military veterans

Law Enforcement Officers

The weight of the badge doesn't come off with the uniform. We unite law enforcement officers with a loyal, vetted canine companion, prepared with care, matched to the person, and supported long after the placement is made.

Learn More about our program for law enforcement officers

With Gratitude

Thank you for your service.

We value your service, and we are deeply grateful for everything you have done for your country.

Whether you served in the military or wore a badge to protect your community, you gave years of your life to something bigger than yourself, and you carried that responsibility with honor. Now we would be proud to carry something for you: a loyal friend who stays by your side for the rest of your life.

  • We honor your service.

  • We respect all that you gave.

  • We stand with you for life.

In Their Words

Stories of Service

  • “

    There was a time when I stopped looking forward to going anywhere. Life had changed, and I wasn't sure how to find my way back to the things I loved. Then K9 Honor Society brought Cooper into my life.

    Now he's waiting by the door every morning, ready for our next adventure. He doesn't care about my wheelchair or the things I can't do. To him, I'm just his best friend.

    K9 Honor Society didn't just help me find a dog. They helped me find a reason to look forward to tomorrow.

    Marcus Williams

    U.S. Army Veteran

  • “

    When I left the Navy, I missed being part of something. I missed the friendships, the routine, and having somewhere I belonged.

    When I connected with K9 Honor Society, I never imagined how much one little dog could change my life.

    Bailey has brought laughter, new friendships, and a little chaos into my everyday routine. Wherever we go, somebody stops to say hello to her.

    I thought I was just getting a companion. What I really found was a whole new chapter of my life.

    Rebecca Miller

    U.S. Navy Veteran

  • “

    I've lived a long life. I've seen a lot, lost people I loved, and learned that sometimes the quietest days can feel the longest.

    Every morning, he sits beside my chair while I drink my coffee. He follows me around the house like we're still young men with places to be.

    We're both getting older, and we both move a little slower these days. But that's all right. We've got nowhere to rush.

    Thanks to K9 Honor Society, these days I don't have to enjoy life's little moments alone.

    Robert Thompson

    U.S. Army Vietnam Veteran

  • “

    After years of service, I knew life was going to change. What I didn't know was how much I'd miss having a sense of purpose and connection.

    K9 Honor Society helped me find Koda, and honestly, life hasn't been quiet since.

    Every time I come home, he runs toward me like I've been gone for years, even if it's only been a few hours. He makes me laugh when I don't feel like smiling.

    I thought I was giving a dog a place in my home. Turns out, he was making a much bigger place for himself in my heart.

    Jasmine Carter

    U.S. Air Force Veteran

  • “

    I used to be the kind of guy who planned everything. In the Marines, you always knew where you needed to be and when.

    Then Rex came along and decided he had his own schedule.

    The first time I took him hiking, we were supposed to be out for thirty minutes. Two hours later, we were still in the woods because he insisted on investigating every tree, every sound, and every squirrel.

    K9 Honor Society told me they'd find a dog that matched my personality. Somehow, they found one even more stubborn than me.

    Rex has taught me something I never learned in uniform: sometimes the best moments happen when you forget the plan and just enjoy the day.

    Michael Anderson

    U.S. Marine Corps Veteran
